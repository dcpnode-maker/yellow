AGENT-INFERRED: - [`accounts.priceViews`](https://developers.google.com/hotels/hotel-prices/api-reference/rest/v3/accounts.priceViews) returns account-scoped `partnerHotelId` itineraries with check-in, nights, price, taxes, fees, currency, and update time. The separate Price Feeds API uploads partner rates to Google.
AGENT-INFERRED: - Ordinary Google hotel results can supply dated quote observations through their intended interface, but no quote was collected in this research. [Google’s public help](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/6276008?hl=en) distinguishes selected-date prices, customized prices, and averages—the latter can be the median over the next 90 days. These must not be converted into a daily calendar or occupancy evidence.
