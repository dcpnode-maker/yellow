## Outcome

Turn Yellow's exact four-skin catalogue into four genuinely different, world-class
experiences. Apply the founder's screenshots and two reference recordings as motion,
depth and hierarchy inspiration while keeping one semantic application, one set of
governed APIs and one stable operational truth.

This order interprets the requested “Steve Jobs” standard as ruthless subtraction,
clear hierarchy, coherent spatial behavior and invisible complexity. It does not mean
decorative excess, copied vendor artwork or animation that slows hotel work.

## Four-skin design contract

1. The selector remains exactly `apple`, `android`, `win95`, `glass`; Apple remains
   the fail-closed default. There is one ordered semantic DOM and one workflow.
2. **Apple** uses severe reduction, precise system typography, generous whitespace,
   solid readable data planes, a restrained translucent command shelf and one obvious
   primary action. Spatial motion is calm, short and spring-like.
3. **Android** is genuinely current Material You: tonal bento groups, 48px controls,
   visible state layers, adaptive navigation, expressive bounded geometry and
   emphasized easing. It must not be Apple with purple paint.
4. **Windows 95/98** is an authentic classic window composition: navy title chrome,
   square grey work areas, outset commands, inset data regions, dotted/yellow focus
   and immediate tactile press. Its fidelity, not modern animation, provides delight.
5. **Glass** is the flagship spatial system derived from the two videos: one stable
   luminous shell, anchored navigation, layered document/workbench depth, content-only
   transitions and point-of-intent status resolution. Dense financial/data planes stay
   opaque and readable; blur is limited to hierarchy surfaces.
6. Reference A contributes the centered sign-in shell, stable outer frame and
   content-only workspace morph. Reference B contributes layered tabs, list-to-detail
   spatial continuity and document depth. Yellow copies no brand, artwork, logo,
   person, text, product layout or trademark from either reference.
7. Status meaning, focus order, deep links, monetary/occupancy warnings, security
   states, drafts and PEEK/DRAWER/WORKBENCH semantics remain identical across skins.

## Motion and performance contract

1. One dependency-free coordinator may animate only the smallest changing region.
   Navigation and property/date context remain stable.
2. Motion tokens are bounded: 90ms press/focus, 150ms peek, 220ms drawer/inline state,
   280ms workbench and at most 400ms for a flagship shared-surface transition.
3. Routine motion uses transform and opacity only. No animated blur, width, height,
   top or left; no scroll-jacking, infinite decoration, permanent `will-change`,
   blocking transition, WebGL or layout-dependent camera effect.
4. Animations are interruptible, stale transitions are cancelled, focus moves only
   when semantic navigation requires it, and API actions never wait for animation.
5. Coarse pointer, page visibility, reduced-motion, forced-colours and missing
   backdrop/View-Transition support receive immediate accessible fallbacks.
6. Combined operator HTML/CSS/JS stays at or below 98,304 gzip bytes. Improvement must
   replace or deduplicate existing styling rather than accumulate payload.
