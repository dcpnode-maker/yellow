**Status:** ACTIVE — founder-directed research and design

**Phase:** Cross-phase product design supporting 7–17
**Owner:** Codex / Astra synthesis, bounded parallel source research
**Risk:** Documentation and design only

## Outcome

Translate the founder's approved architecture and staff-first hotel/STR ecosystem
direction into sourced product requirements, concrete journeys and implementable
acceptance criteria. Benchmark Beds24 and major PMSs; document supplier-side OTA
integration access for India, UAE, US and Europe; compare low-cost multilingual voice
and RMS technologies/data. Preserve the 18-phase scope and recorded 11→13→17 priority
subject to implementation dependencies. Research completion is not feature completion.

## Required results

1. Primary-source PMS comparison, with Beds24 examined for hotel and STR workflows;
   publicly documented capability distinguished from observed screenshots and inference.
2. Reservation detail families and branch matrix; Today/future arrivals drill-down;
   guest/reservation continuity; contextual cashier billing; checkout to housekeeping,
   room inspection, minibar and bell desk; dirty/clean/inspected as distinct concepts.
3. OTA supply/connectivity versus demand/affiliate distinction, partner gates,
   per-channel capability negotiation, mapping/version/retry/reconciliation contracts.
4. Multilingual voice read and action flows, ambiguity resolution, role/property
   checks and existing domain commands; accuracy/latency evaluation and privacy controls.
5. RMS internal/external signal plan, source licences/access/cost limits, time-aware
   backtesting, net contribution objectives and bounded publication through rate controls.
6. Distinct material/layout systems with progressive disclosure, accessible motion,
   compact operational hierarchy and measurable interface performance budgets.
7. Trace each requirement to existing code or an explicit planned gap and owning phase.
   No invented implementation, integration availability, benchmark or market-share claim.

## Boundaries

No partner outreach, purchases, credential access, new dependencies, model downloads,
live hotel data, database writes, migrations, runtime changes, deployment or merge.
Subsequent code changes need their own bounded implementation scope and proportionate
proof. Preserve existing design reference provenance and historical approvals.
