## Objective

Promote the independently accepted Order565 frontend and prove one bounded fictional
cashier charge through the actual public Yellow conversation without disturbing any
other public data or losing the request's immutable financial evidence.

## Scope

- exact accepted Order565 frontend asset promotion
- restricted current public PostgreSQL checkpoint and read-only pre/post fingerprints
- one fictional SAR 25 Laundry charge to the current Omar Siddiqui stay through the
  actual rendered Yellow proposal and separate affirmative confirmation
- exact idempotent replay/reconciliation proof for that same request only
- `handoff/reviews/566-public-yellow-cashier-posting-release.md`

## Execution gates

1. Before any public write, create a new inheritance-protected consistent archive,
   readable catalogue and SHA-256 receipt. Capture all-table counts/fingerprints and
   exact current reservation, sole open folio, zero/known balance, catalogue, actor,
   grant, open business day and current app/container/tunnel identities.
2. Independently verify the accepted Order565 hashes, current public target, exact
   fictional scenario and expected deltas. Drift or ambiguity stops execution.
3. Promote only the accepted app source; database, PostgreSQL, Valkey and tunnel stay
   running. Verify local/public health and exact served JS/CSS hashes before posting.
4. In the actual public 375px UI, type `post a SAR 25 laundry charge to Omar Siddiqui`.
   Verify the exact guest, confirmation, folio, Laundry class, SAR 25.00 and quantity
   1 are visible and that no charge exists yet. Then type a separate `yes` once.
5. Preserve the original idempotency key/body/receipt without credentials. Reconcile
   the returned journal through the refreshed statement. An identical-key/body replay
   may be used only to prove no duplicate effect; never generate a replacement key for
   an uncertain outcome.
6. Independent non-implementing postflight must personally prove exactly one new
   balanced charge journal, two posting lines, one fact/outbox/idempotency effect,
   correct folio balance/statement and unchanged protected/unrelated tables and
   occupancy. It must also verify current public health and rendered cashier result.

## Stop conditions

- stale/closed/multiple folios, ineligible reservation, wrong currency or route
- missing/changed grant, sealed business day or failed checkpoint
- unexpected preexisting charge, non-matching receipt, duplicate journal, unbalanced
  lines, any fiscal/payment/document effect, unrelated row drift or public health loss

## Exclusions

- No correction, reversal, allowance, transfer, split, settlement, payment, invoice,
  tax/fiscal issue, restore, erasure, schema/configuration change or whole-PMS claim.
