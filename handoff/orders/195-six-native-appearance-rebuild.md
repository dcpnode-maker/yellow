## Six-system contract

1. One ordered semantic application and exact governed commands remain underneath all
   appearances. Appearance changes presentation, geometry and bounded motion only.
2. **Apple iOS** follows current first-party Apple hierarchy: large-title continuity,
   grouped/inset planes, precise system controls, bottom/split navigation adaptation,
   spatial sheets, native-feeling press/spring behavior and ruthless reduction. It is
   not generic rounded ERP cards and uses no copied Apple asset or logo.
3. **Pixel Android** follows current Pixel/Material3 Expressive grammar: edge-to-edge
   composition, 48px controls, adaptive navigation bar/rail, tonal containers, state
   layers, expressive shape hierarchy and predictive spatial continuity. It is not
   Apple recoloured.
4. **Windows95/98** uses authentic application chrome: taskbar, Start affordance,
   title/menu/status strips, explicit MDI grid areas, exact bevel/pressed/focus states
   and immediate interaction. The active window starts directly below its chrome;
   no empty implicit grid region above it may exceed 32px.
5. **Glassmorphism** follows the founder's Glass UI Kit reference through an original
   Yellow luminous environment: deep cobalt/navy field, distinct cyan/indigo/accent
   light volumes, dark navigation glass, translucent content glass, specular edges,
   three visible depth planes and opaque readable financial/table glass. Blur alone
   is not acceptance.
6. **Neomorphism** is a complete coherent-light tactile system: convex, concave,
   pressed, raised, focus, error and disabled states. Shadows never replace readable
   borders, contrast or hierarchy.
7. **Enterprise ERP** combines information-dense hotel operations with a restrained
   monochrome/blue analytic hierarchy inspired by the supplied ERP and analytics
   references: compact rail, command bar, KPI/bento summaries, legible dense tables,
   filters and drill-down continuity. It is not the fallback geometry for other
   appearances.
8. Inventory/room configuration may use an original CSS perspective room/space
   navigator inspired by the supplied 3D apartment reference, but it remains a
   presentation of existing server truth. No mock room, new API, WebGL, asset copy or
   client-owned availability/configuration authority is allowed.

## Interaction and performance contract

- Secondary workspaces open as an anchored overlay/sheet with focus containment and
  Escape/return-focus behavior. Its toggle changes `.domain-bar` and workbench Y
  geometry by at most 1px.
- Routine transitions animate transform/opacity only: 90ms press, 180–220ms local
  state and 260–320ms workspace transitions. No animated blur/layout property,
  scroll-jacking, permanent `will-change` or long task over 50ms.
- Reduced motion, coarse pointer, forced colours and no-backdrop support fail to
  immediate solid readable states. Targets are at least 44px; Android is 48px.
- D-526 retires the obsolete 98,304-byte combined visual-shell ceiling. Payload is
  measured and reviewed for latency, caching and duplication, but world-class visual
  quality is not rejected solely by that historical byte cap.
