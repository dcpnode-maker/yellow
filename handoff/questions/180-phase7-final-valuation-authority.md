# Question 180 — Phase-7 final valuation and invoice-buyer authority

**Status:** RESOLVED-D991
**Raised by:** Codex post-Order341 Phase-7 evidence audit
**Date:** 2026-09-02

## Why founder product intent is required

Approved Order341 proves only quoted per-room-night GST rate applicability. It
explicitly excludes CGST Act Section15 final taxable value. Current authoritative
data does not record:

- the moment/source at which price becomes actually paid or payable;
- the legal invoice-window buyer rather than a candidate guest/company;
- unrelated/distinct-person and sole-money-consideration evidence;
- per-room-night package, promotion, discount, fee and Section15 addition allocation;
- Section15(3) discount eligibility; or
- Rules27–35, pure-agent, special-supply and tax-inclusive branch applicability.

Reservation, folio and generic posting rows cannot safely substitute for those facts.
Missing evidence must not be interpreted as a negative answer, and caller booleans
must not become statutory authority.

## Recommended founder decision

Approve this complete default, or replace any numbered clause:

1. **Finalization source:** a governed fiscal-issue request locks one exact folio
   window/reservation and appends immutable per-room-night valuation evidence derived
   from governed room/package/discount/fee posting roots. No browser amount is final.
2. **Buyer:** the fiscal-issue requester explicitly designates one eligible persisted
   Party as the legal invoice-window buyer; a different authorized user is required
   only when designation conflicts with the reservation payer/company relationship.
3. **Ordinary Section15 path:** require explicit persisted evidence that parties are
   not related/distinct and money is the sole consideration. Absence fails closed.
4. **Allocation:** every governed package/promotion/discount/fee and Section15(2)
   addition is allocated deterministically across exact room nights before tax;
   Section15(3) discounts reduce value only with persisted eligibility evidence.
5. **Special cases:** related/distinct, non-money, pure-agent, special-supply,
   tax-inclusive or otherwise indeterminable cases return `manual_valuation_required`
   and cannot issue/fiscalize until a later governed specialist workflow supplies the
   prescribed valuation evidence.
6. **Corrections:** final valuation evidence is append-only; later changes supersede
   with explicit lineage and drive corrective fiscal documents/postings, never edits.

## Consequence

After resolution, the minimum Phase-7 sequence is final valuation evidence → taxable
value composer → component tax/rounding/totals → semantic routing → governed posting
and correction → invoice buyer/payload → gapless hash-chained document issue → fiscal
submission state → IRP sandbox round-trip → operator journey and exit gate.

Until then, no agent may calculate final GST money from Order341 quote evidence,
`price_override`, folio balance, generic posting lines or untyped JSON.

## Resolution

On 2026-09-02 the founder explicitly replied **“approve both recommended policies”**.
Clauses 1–6 above are binding product policy under D991. Phase-7 final valuation may
now begin with append-only governed evidence; tax money and fiscal issue remain later
bounded orders.
