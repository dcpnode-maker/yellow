### Bigint-only money, overflow and currency

- Authoring accepts canonical decimal minor-unit strings and converts once to bigint;
  tagged persistence rejects unsafe numbers and signed-range overflow.
- Evaluator and composition arithmetic uses bigint, explicit half-up basis-point
  rounding and checked add/multiply paths. Package, evaluator and rate-plan currencies
  must match exact uppercase three-letter currency codes.
- Tests use values beyond JavaScript's safe-integer range, reject floats, malformed
  decimal strings, cross-currency composition and signed-range overflow, and preserve
  exact minor-unit evidence through transport.

### Targeting, composition and historical evidence

- Target resolution is deterministic, equal top tuples conflict, and exact
  property/commercial references are tenant-owned.
- Composition keeps room price, package allocation, promotions, guest/policy,
  restriction, operational-block and distribution evidence distinct. It cannot create
  availability or tax results.
- Prior price/model references retain exact identity and version. Published release
  content is frozen; reuse copies a new unsaved draft and undo publishes a new version
  rather than editing history.

### Four-eyes approval, publication and undo

- The requester cannot self-approve. Approval binds exact simulation/content hashes
  and becomes stale after a newer draft.
- Twenty concurrent publishers activate once. A late outbox failure restores the prior
  active version and leaves no partial bulk publication.
- Rejection is terminal. Undo copies immutable history into a new draft and repeats the
  distinct-operator approval path before publication.

### RMS economics boundary

`src/contexts/rates/economics.ts` is a pure, frozen calculation/transport contract. It
accepts only named non-negative signed-range bigint costs in one currency, defines gross
room revenue as tax-exclusive, exposes exact quotient/remainder ratios, keeps negative
contribution visible, and compares the bid price to contribution rather than subtracting
displacement twice. Checked intermediate arithmetic rejects signed overflow.

The contract contains no transaction, repository, event, recommendation, persistence,
publication, accounting journal, tax calculation or operational command. Its evidence
encoder accepts only the canonical immutable snapshot and emits decimal strings. Thus
Order 091 establishes vocabulary and arithmetic without acquiring accounting, tax or
rate/occupancy authority.
