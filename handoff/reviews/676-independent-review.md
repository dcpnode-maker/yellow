## Live timestamp-precision correction — independent addendum

Root's intercepted current-app read found a real mismatch after the initial review: reservation detail serialized `segment.to` as `2026-09-25T11:00:00.000000Z`, while the segment lookup serialized `period.to` as `2026-09-25T11:00:00.000Z`. The initial component compared these strings directly, so it could reject an applied command or misclassify an unchanged stay despite the same instant. That was a real contract defect in the initially reviewed client; root did not claim success from the intercepted 503/409 recovery exercise and no live PATCH reached the server.

I independently inspected the correction in `reservation-departure-change.ts` and `ReservationDepartureChange.tsx`. `sameRecordedInstant` validates offset-bearing timestamps, retains the fractional microsecond remainder beyond JavaScript's millisecond parsing, and compares the resulting microsecond instants. The component now requires matching exact reservation, confirmation and segment identities and matching start/end instants in both authoritative readbacks before success or unchanged classification. The local no-op guard also uses this comparison. The original lookup's `expectedPeriod` strings remain byte-unchanged in the outbound payload and frozen retry attempt. I checked the permanent regression for six-digit/three-digit and equivalent-offset equality, nonzero microsecond inequality, and six-digit unchanged-time rejection. The mounted browser fixture now supplies six-digit detail timestamps against three-digit segment timestamps.
