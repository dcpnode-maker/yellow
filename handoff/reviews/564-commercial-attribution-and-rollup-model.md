## Direction accepted

Reuse org_node, Party identity, reservation commercial codes, immutable ledger evidence and governed extensions before inventing tables. MSG→MS should remain its own classification; source/channel and company/booker are independent intersections rather than compulsory descendants. Client examples are configuration, not global enums. Alternate brand/geography sets must not change authorization containment. Additive numerators and recomputed ratios, explicit Unmapped, currency separation and no pre-review public mutation are correct principles.

These principles do not yet constitute an implementable reporting contract. The following corrections are required before architecture acceptance.

## Required corrections

### 1. Separate measure grains and publish metric eligibility

Draft§4 puts `rooms_available` on each demand/revenue leaf, then sums it. A property/date denominator copied onto MS/company/reservation leaves fans out:100 available rooms and two sold nights in different segments become200 available and1% occupancy instead of100 and2%. Keep separate inventory-date, stay-night and posting-line measures; aggregate each at its own unique grain before joining compatible totals. Define an eligibility matrix: occupancy/RevPAR only for disjoint supported inventory scopes; demand-only leaves return null/not-applicable unless a separately named contribution-to-property-capacity measure is deliberately requested. ADR may be computed at demand scope only with coherent sold-night/revenue policies.

Do not use availability_projection.available (physical minus sold/held/blocked/OOO) as the capacity denominator. It is remaining availability, not available room-nights. The existing occupancySignal is a sellability signal including held demand, not historical hotel occupancy. State exact supported denominator authority, OOO/OOS policy, date coverage and missing-history failure. Beds, whole rooms and composite suites share spaces; blindly summing unit_type capacity double-counts mutually exclusive configurations. Either define a proven common inventory counting basis or explicitly reject those combined KPI scopes. Currency must appear in the mapped grain, not only response prose; never repeat room nights per currency bucket without a documented eligibility rule.

### 2. Resolve historical semantics before calling a mode “as booked”

Draft§3.6 freezes attribution at commit, but§3.7 chooses the version effective on every subsequent stay/posting date. Those are different reports. Specify separate functions for pinned booking evidence, effective-on-business-date attribution if desired, and explicit selected-version restatement. Define treatment of subsequent overrides, knowledge-time cutoff, backdated versions, unmapped legacy reservations, changed org paths and company/product relationships. Do not reconstruct booking-time truth from mutable current columns or silently apply current taxonomy to old stays.

Reuse extension.id/version/effective/status and fact_log recorded_at/valid_from/supersedes explicitly; avoid duplicate authoritative version/effective fields inside JSON. The current ExtensionService version constructor locks its key but does not implement the proposed activation/non-overlap/graph validation workflow, and recordFact stamps transaction time. The design must name governed owner commands, expected-version/CAS, scope, idempotency, activation lock and same-Tx fact/outbox evidence. Explain how future date semantics fit the existing helper without inventing a second temporal spine. An immutable successor with an open-ended predecessor needs an unambiguous effective-selection rule, not merely a promise of non-overlap.

### 3. Correct the product and relationship Natural-Solution Test

Existing D-240/D-262 and `rates/targeting.ts` define a class as a version-local snapshot of one or more unit_type IDs; physical specificity is property→class→unit_type→sellable. The draft instead makes class a child of unit_type and places bed configuration on the same chain. Resolve that conflict explicitly. A bed configuration can vary across units and need not be a hierarchy parent. Reuse the established class concept, or distinctly name an independent analytical classification with no change to pricing authority. `sellable_unit_space` is many-to-many: a suite maps to several spaces, and a space can back mutually exclusive sellables. It is not an ordinary single-parent additive tree.

The draft omits existing `party_relationship` (`employee_of`, `subsidiary_of`, `books_for`), `reservation_group.account_party`, and the rate-target class snapshot in its reuse assessment. Specify their roles and limits: current relationships do not supply historical effective dating or uniquely choose one company for a booker with several relationships. Explicit company attribution must be governed, never guessed from primary guest/GST profile. Legal company, ultimate parent and individual booker need separate identifiers/cardinality. A channel account must not be inferred from channel_code alone. Define code-binding uniqueness per tenant/property/dimension/version and fail closed on duplicate/conflicting bindings.

### 4. Define ledger-side revenue and association lineage exactly

Actual `financials/postings.ts` writes guest debit +amount with folio_id and revenue credit -amount with NULL folio_id. Joining only folio-bearing lines misses the revenue side; summing both balances to zero. Define revenue as sign-normalized eligible revenue-account posting lines once per posting ID, then resolve only proven journal/folio/reservation associations. Show exact mappings for the two-line charge and supported multi-line/tax/correction cases; ambiguous multi-folio, house/outlet/event or unassociated revenue must remain explicit unallocated/non-stay revenue, not dropped or duplicated. A folio is an account window and reservation_id is nullable.

Preserve original and reversal lines and net them by signed economics; never discard both or double-negate. Quantity is descriptive and is not multiplied (D-323). Do not classify historical revenue by a mutable current tx_code USALI label without a pinned classification policy. For posting dates outside a stay, room moves, multiple products and retrospective corrections, a posting has no automatic unique room-night/product: define approved attribution or Unmapped rather than multiplying it across segments. Keep posted revenue separate from quoted/OTB revenue and decide whether ADR is posted-business-date or stay-consumption based. Founder-controlled room-revenue/complimentary/house-use policies may stay unresolved, but affected production metrics must remain unavailable.

### 5. Define room-night eligibility and overlapping-set totals

There is no `reservation_segment.superseded_by` in the actual schema. Segments have seq/status/period and moves create new legs; the query plan's “unsuperseded eligible segments” needs a real predicate and dated evidence. Define planned sold nights versus actual occupied nights, same-date room moves, partial-day stays, cancellation after partial occupancy, early departure and no-show. One leaf per segment/date can count two nights for one mid-day room move. Preserve historical occupied nights after later reservation state changes; current reservation status alone cannot decide them.

Alternate sets can overlap within the same version, not only across versions. Use semi-join membership for union filters and deduplicate property IDs. Label independently overlapping group rows non-additive, or constrain a requested roll-up to a partition; do not sum overlapping sets into a grand total. Historical operating path versus current authorization path also needs explicit separation: taxonomy membership never confers access.

### 6. Make the API and executable proof plan sufficiently precise

Name the reporting context public surface and exact report/PII permissions. Tenant comes from verified session; authorization must be intersected per property before aggregation and rechecked on drill. Define whether partially authorized subtrees are rejected or visibly partial. Bind opaque filter/cursor tokens to tenant, authorized scope, immutable taxonomy hash/version set, mode, dates, currency, ordering and knowledge/projection cutoff; tampering or changed grants must not reveal hidden rows. Company/booker drill-down should not expose contacts, KYC or unnecessary guest identity.

Specify half-open property-local date inputs, mixed-timezone interpretation, group/filter OR-within/AND-across behavior, stable keyset sort, maximum range/group cardinality, Unmapped identifiers, null reason codes, ratio precision/rounding and total-versus-page semantics. A multi-version “as booked” report cannot return one misleading scalar taxonomy version. Snapshot/freshness must keep cards and drill totals consistent across concurrent changes. No floats or JS Number conversion for minor-unit sums; SQL sum(bigint) can exceed int64, so bound or preserve exact decimal aggregation explicitly.

The existing `stats_daily` has no currency, company, MSG, hierarchy version or provenance column. Its apparently nullable dimension columns participate in a primary key and are actually NOT NULL. `OperatingPerformanceService` explicitly returns `scenario_reporting_projection`, clamps sold to available and returns zero ratios on zero denominators. It is not evidence of an implemented audited operational revenue projection or a compatible metric kernel. Preserve its bounded existing contract; do not silently reuse its synthetic plan defaults, clamping or zero-denominator behavior for this new API.

### Residual corrections required

1. **Order/draft disagreement:** Order564 still requires product `room type/product → room class/bed configuration` and expresses MSG/MS/source/company as a chain. Amend the governing order to the corrected class-snapshot→type path and independent demand/distribution/account dimensions; the linked draft must not silently override its order. 2. **Reversal query contradiction:** draft§7.3 says “Select sign-normalized eligible revenue-account credits once per posting line”. Full reversal revenue lines are debits. Name a hotel-night grain uniquely keyed by reservation/business-date (within tenant/property/counting basis) and separate allocation legs whose exact weights sum to one, or an equally precise conserved design. Product allocation without approved evidence/policy remains Unmapped/unavailable. Do not claim that separately summing unit-weight segment rows conserves one night.
