# Preliminary app-name shortlist

**Status:** preliminary naming research for Order440, 2026-09-05. These are
working proposals, not selected names. This bounded web review checks obvious
exact-name use in hospitality and software; it is not a trademark opinion,
company-name clearance, domain-availability claim or linguistic clearance.

## What the name needs to do

Yellow is becoming a hospitality operating system: one product for hotels and
short-term rentals, with work spanning the guest journey and every department.
The name should therefore feel human to a front-desk agent, housekeeper, owner
and guest; remain broad enough for operations, finance and revenue work; and be
easy to say after hearing it once. India is the starting context, but the word
should travel without requiring knowledge of one language or one lodging type.

## Shortlist and collision notes

| Preliminary name | Pronunciation and product fit | Bounded collision finding | Initial view |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hospello** | *hos-PELL-oh*. Combines hospitality with “hello”; friendly, compact and broad enough for hotel, STR and departmental work. | No obvious exact hospitality/software operator surfaced in the searches run for this note. The exact `.com` currently resolves to a [registrar sale page](https://www.hospello.com/), which proves neither rights nor future availability. | **Lead candidate.** Strong verbal welcome and a credible master brand; test whether some speakers hear “hospital.” |
| **GuestSpring** | *guest-spring*. Suggests a source of service and a system that helps teams respond. “Guest” keeps the operational breadth tied to a clear purpose. | No obvious exact hospitality/software owner surfaced in this bounded search. That absence is uncertainty, not evidence of availability. “Spring” is common in software language, so search distinctiveness may be weaker than the exact phrase suggests. | **Lead candidate.** Warm and legible, though slightly longer and more descriptive. |
| **GuestThread** | *guest-thread*. Expresses the product idea well: the guest journey is the thread connecting reservations, rooms, service, finance and ownership. | No obvious exact hospitality brand surfaced, but “guest thread” is established technical wording in virtual-machine and emulator logs, visible for example in an [open-source issue](https://github.com/Ryubing/Issues/issues/144). That creates unrelated search noise. | **Promising concept name.** Excellent internal metaphor; less warm and ownable as a public brand than the leaders. |
| **Yellow** | *YELL-oh*. Familiar, cheerful and already carries project continuity. | **Welora** | *weh-LOR-ah*. Sounds welcoming and international without describing only rooms or bookings. | Active technology uses are already visible: [Welora](https://welora.bz/) offers mobile-app development and [Welora AI](https://welora.ai/) is a visual brand studio. | **Do not advance now.** Pleasant sound, but the technology-name field is already busy. |
| **Stayloom** | *stay-loom*. ## Recommendation

Advance **Hospello** and **GuestSpring** to a small naming test. Hospello is the
stronger master-brand candidate: short, friendly and not tied to a single module.
GuestSpring is the clearer promise candidate: it keeps every department oriented
toward the guest while still allowing labels such as “GuestSpring Operations” or
“GuestSpring Finance.” Test both unprompted in spoken English and Hindi, asking
people to repeat, spell and describe what they expect the product to do.

Keep **Yellow** as the continuity choice during that test. Renaming carries real
documentation and recognition cost, but the nearby enterprise-software uses mean
continuity alone is not a sound reason to make Yellow the final public brand.
GuestThread can remain a product narrative or workflow term even if it is not the
company name: one guest thread, many accountable handoffs.

Before selection, commission proper trademark and company-name searches in India
and intended launch markets, check app stores and relevant domains directly, and
run native-speaker checks in priority languages for pronunciation, unintended
meanings and transcription. Repeat the collision search immediately before a
decision; names, domains and products can change after this dated review.
