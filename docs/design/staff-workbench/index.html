A hotel shift, connected

This is a working interaction study for Yellow, using fictional people and hotel operations. It does not connect to the hotel database, send messages, post charges, issue keys or invoices, or change a real booking. Reloading resets all actions.

Try the early arrival: Front office requests preparation. Switch to Housekeeping, accept the handoff, complete cleaning and inspection. Switch back to Front office, acknowledge readiness and complete the arrival review.

Department selection explores a design role; it is not authentication. The production app must authorize every query and command on the server. Event, F&B and spa workflows here are planned scope.

The three workspace skins share the same tasks and evidence: Calm Workbench, Precision Desk and Service Timeline. Change the skin while completing a checklist: your entered work stays in place. The production app retains its six appearance families.

Guest and staff identity cards take their surface direction from the right-hand Sophie Bennett card by Tran Mau Tri Tam for UI8. The portraits and identities here are fictional; they are not the designer’s portrait or real hotel records. Department navigation explores a role and does not grant access.
